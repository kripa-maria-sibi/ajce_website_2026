How to Apply

Complete your application in five clear steps.

1

Prepare Documents

Collect marksheets, certificates, and identity proof before beginning the application.

2

Submit Application

Fill out the form with accurate details and submit required information.

3

Verification

Academic and entrance credentials are verified by the admissions team.

4

Offer Confirmation

Selected applicants receive confirmation and enrollment instructions.

5

Enroll

Complete fee payment and formalities to confirm your admission.

Required Documents

Keep these documents ready before applying:

  • 10+2 marksheet or diploma certificate (as applicable)
  • Transfer certificate and conduct certificate
  • Entrance scorecard (if applicable)
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Government ID proof (Aadhaar or Passport)
